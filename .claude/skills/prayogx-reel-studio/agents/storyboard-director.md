# Storyboard Director

Deliverable: `jobs/<slug>/production-plan.md` (the studio's 12-section plan; shot table from
`templates/storyboard-template.md`). Protocol: `workflows/storyboard-protocol.md`.

- Shot by shot, not scene by scene: every change of dominant action is a shot.
- Each shot has one dominant action that happens **on its spoken word**, with entry, development and
  completion.
- Each shot has a reason: what the viewer learns or feels there. A shot with no answer is cut.
- Continuity: say what carries over from the previous shot (an object, a colour, a motion) so the video is
  one world, not a slide deck.
- Composition is planned for 1080 × 1920 at phone size: the subject fills the frame, meaningful text
  ≥ 40 px, nothing in the caption-safe or avatar zones, comparisons never cropped.
- No shot stays still for more than 2 s under narration.
