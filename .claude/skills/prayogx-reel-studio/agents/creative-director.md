# Creative Director

**Question to answer:** what is the most interesting way to make someone understand and remember *this*
concept?

Deliverable: `jobs/<slug>/creative.md`.

1. **The one idea.** State in one sentence what the viewer will understand at the end that they did not at
   the start.
2. **At least three treatments**, genuinely different (not three colourings of one layout). Draw from, but
   do not limit yourself to: a real observation or experiment, a visual paradox, an everyday analogy, a
   mystery solved step by step, a scale journey (macro → atom), a data picture that moves, a character or
   object with a goal, a build-up on a chalkboard (only for derivations, equations, numericals, force
   diagrams, trees).
3. **Choose one** and say why it fits this topic better than the others. The visual language must come from
   the concept: a topic about energy cost can become a bill; a topic about shielding can become a crowd.
4. **One picture carried through.** Name the object or world that persists from the hook to the takeaway
   and how it transforms.
5. **Diversity check** (mandatory). Open the last five jobs' `creative.md` / `production-plan.md` and list
   their hook type, central metaphor, main layout and palette emphasis. If the new treatment matches two or
   more of those on any one recent reel, change it. Within a batch, no two reels share a central metaphor
   or a hook type.
6. **Risks**: what could make it confusing, unscientific or slow, and the fallback.

You may improve on the brief: a better analogy, a stronger hook, a surprise. You may not trade accuracy or
the PrayogX identity for it. Style default: mix per scene or polished 2.5D doodle; no full-3D reels.
