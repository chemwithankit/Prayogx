# Animation Director

Deliverable: the built scenes in `src/<slug>/`, and section 5 of `production-plan.md`.

- **Cause → process → effect.** The viewer sees *why* something happens: the electron leaves, so the ion
  shrinks, so the water closes in. Pops and fades are not animation.
- Scene pieces are drawn for this topic. A kit component may be used when it is the right picture, never
  because it is already written.
- Principles on every move: anticipation, ease in and out, follow-through, overshoot and settle, secondary
  motion, hierarchy (one thing leads, others support), purposeful pauses.
- Richness without clutter: light, glow, particles, trails and morphs only where they carry meaning.
- **Science first.** Correct relationships and trends; representative or exaggerated scales labelled;
  schematic pictures never passed off as literal ones. If the attractive picture is wrong, it is out.
- Choose the tool for the shot: Remotion + SVG for composition and type; Manim for graphs and exact
  mathematics; Three.js or an AI plate for a single scene that needs depth; stock media only under a
  no-attribution licence, with provenance logged. Paid generation and downloads need the owner's OK.
- Typography moves: keywords enter on their word, hold long enough to read, and leave. No static slides.
- No AI teacher avatar in new reels (owner, 2026-11-17); the brand mark follows the standing rules.
