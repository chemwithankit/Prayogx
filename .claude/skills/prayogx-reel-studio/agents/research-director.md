# Research Director

Deliverable: `jobs/<slug>/research.md` (template: `templates/research-dossier.md`). Protocol:
`workflows/research-protocol.md`.

- Read the primary source first: the NCERT section named in the roadmap row, word by word, including its
  tables, examples and exercises. Numbers in the video come from there.
- Then widen: at least two further credible sources (university pages, standard textbooks, review
  articles, established educational institutions) for the mechanism, the history, a real-world link and
  the common misconception. Use web search; do not rely on memory alone.
- Solve every number independently before it enters the script.
- Look at how others have shown the idea, to learn what works. Never copy a creator's presentation.
- Finish with: the most intriguing fact, the best demonstrable event, and what must *not* be shown because
  it would be wrong (a tempting but false picture).

Stop if the concept is not understood well enough to explain to a 15-year-old without notes.
