# Storyboard protocol

Output: `jobs/<slug>/production-plan.md` (the studio's 12 sections: creative direction, story, storyboard,
camera, animation rules, kinetic typography, VFX, sound, transitions, timeline, tech, QA) with the shot
table from `templates/storyboard-template.md`, plus `sound-plan.md`.

For every shot:

1. number, scene and duration (from the voice timings once they exist);
2. the narration words that cue it;
3. composition: what fills the frame, where, at what size;
4. the primary visual element;
5. the animation: entry → development → completion, and the cause it shows;
6. camera move and its purpose;
7. transition in and out (what carries over);
8. typography: the keyword, its entrance, hold and exit;
9. VFX, each with its meaning;
10. SFX and what the music does;
11. background and lighting;
12. the intended effect on the viewer (learn / feel).

Checks before approving the storyboard:

- Cover the narration column: do the pictures alone tell the story? Cover the pictures: does the narration
  alone make sense? Both must hold.
- Count the dominant actions: fewer than one per ~4 s of narration means slides.
- List the camera moves: at least four different kinds in a reel that uses the camera at all.
- The first frame would stop a scroll; the last frame is the takeaway.
- The diversity check in `creative.md` is done.
