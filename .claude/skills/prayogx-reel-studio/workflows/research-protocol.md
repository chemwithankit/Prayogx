# Research protocol

Output: `jobs/<slug>/research.md` from `templates/research-dossier.md`.

1. **Scope** from the roadmap row: the NCERT section, the one idea, what belongs to neighbouring reels.
2. **Primary source**: the official NCERT text for that section (tables, solved examples, intext and
   exercise questions). Quote section and table numbers. PDFs are never committed.
3. **Widen** with web search: mechanism in more depth than the reel needs, history or discovery, a
   real-world application, a counterintuitive fact, the standard misconception, and how the idea is
   usually visualised. Prefer university and textbook sources; note the URL and what was taken from each.
4. **Verify**: every number solved or cross-checked in a second source; every "always / only / never"
   tested for exceptions (state them or soften the claim).
5. **Visual possibilities**: list real events that can be *shown* (a reaction, a graph that bends, an
   object that fails), and tempting pictures that are scientifically wrong.
6. **The hook candidates**: three true, surprising observations or questions.
7. **Fact sheet**: the short list of statements and numbers the script may use. The script uses nothing
   outside it.

Other creators' videos are references for what communicates, never a source to reproduce. Nothing is
downloaded without the owner's OK.
