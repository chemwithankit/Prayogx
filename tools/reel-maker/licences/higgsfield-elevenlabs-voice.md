## Findings

- **Commercial use of outputs** (Higgsfield §4.4): Higgsfield claims no ownership of inputs or outputs and does not
  restrict their commercial use; the rights survive cancellation. This covers monetized Instagram and YouTube content
  and promotional use, at the Higgsfield level.
- **Provider policies also apply** (Higgsfield §8): for a model provided by a third party, that provider's acceptable-
  or prohibited-use policy applies as well, and where it is stricter it governs. For this asset that is ElevenLabs.
- **Disclosure** (ElevenLabs policy): organizations must clearly disclose AI-generated voice to their audience, and
  must not deceive anyone about whether a voice is AI. Higgsfield §5 adds disclosure where the law requires it. PrayogX
  meets both with the standard line in `voice_profiles.json` → `disclosureText`, on every narrated reel.
- **Consent and impersonation** (Higgsfield §4.2, §5; ElevenLabs policy): no cloning or replication of a person's voice
  without consent; no impersonation. The profile uses a Higgsfield preset voice; nobody's voice is cloned.
- **Provenance marking** (Higgsfield §6.4): outputs may carry invisible watermarks or C2PA provenance data; these must
  not be removed.
- **Training** (Higgsfield §4.4): inputs and outputs may be used to train Higgsfield's models (not on enterprise
  agreements). Narration scripts are public teaching content.
- **Attribution:** none required by either source.
- **Geography** (Higgsfield §17): no country exclusion; local law is the user's responsibility.

## Not covered by this record

Other Higgsfield voice engines (Inworld, Qwen, CosyVoice, Seed Speech, MiniMax) were not reviewed and are not
licensed by this record. Microsoft VibeVoice is excluded: Microsoft recommends it for research and development only.
