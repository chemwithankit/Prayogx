Key points

  • Iodine and NaOH act on compounds with a CH₃CO group.
  • The 3 H atoms of the methyl group are replaced by iodine, giving CI₃.
  • The C–C bond breaks: yellow iodoform, CHI₃, and a carboxylate with 1 carbon fewer.
  • Positive: ethanal, methyl ketones, and ethanol or propan-2-ol, which oxidise to them.
  • Negative: methanol, propanal, pentan-3-one.
