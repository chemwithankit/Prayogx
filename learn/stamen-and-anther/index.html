Key points

  • A stamen has a long, thin filament and an anther at the top.
  • A typical anther has 2 lobes with 2 chambers each: 4 microsporangia.
  • The microsporangium wall has 4 layers: epidermis, endothecium, middle layers and tapetum.
  • The innermost tapetum nourishes the developing pollen grains.
  • The sporogenous tissue in the centre gives rise to pollen grains.
