We see things because light bounces off them into our eyes. At a surface light has 2 options: bounce back (reflection) or go in and bend (refraction).

Key points

  • We see non-luminous objects by the light they reflect into our eyes.
  • Reflection: light returns into the same medium (mirrors).
  • Refraction: light bends on entering another medium because its speed changes (lenses).
  • Part 1: mirrors + reflection; Part 2: refraction + lenses. Your eye has a lens too.

Two options at a surface

BehaviourWhat happensExample
ReflectionLight returns into the same mediumMirror; image in a spoon
RefractionLight bends as its speed changesPencil in water looks broken; lenses

What the chapter covers

  • Part 1: laws of reflection, plane and spherical mirrors, image formation, mirror formula.
  • Part 2: laws of refraction, refractive index, lenses, lens formula, power of a lens.
