Why Do Fingers Wrinkle in Water?

Video not playing? YouTube

Skin soaking up water is not the full story: a finger with nerve damage stays smooth. How the nervous system makes wrinkles, and why their purpose is still debated.

Key points

  • Fingers with nerve damage do not wrinkle normally, so wrinkling is not just water absorption.
  • After a few minutes in water, the nervous system makes blood vessels in the fingertip narrow.
  • Fingertip volume falls and the skin is pulled down into wrinkles.
  • A 2013 study found wet objects were moved faster with wrinkled fingers (reported as about 12%).
  • A 2014 study could not repeat this, so the purpose is still an open question.

What the two studies found

StudyResult
Kareklas, Nettle & Smulders, 2013Wet objects moved faster with wrinkled fingers; no difference for dry objects
Haseleu et al., 2014Could not reproduce the benefit
