Key points

  • The carbonyl carbon is sp², planar; C=O is polar (O δ⁻, C δ⁺), so nucleophiles attack the carbon.
  • Made by oxidation of alcohols, ozonolysis of alkenes, hydration of alkynes, Rosenmund reduction of acyl chlorides.
  • Nucleophilic addition: HCN → cyanohydrin, alcohols → acetal, NH₂–Z → oxime, hydrazone; α-H → aldol, no α-H → Cannizzaro.
  • Tollens' silver mirror and Fehling's red precipitate for aldehydes; carboxylate resonance makes RCOOH acidic, EWGs raise acidity.
