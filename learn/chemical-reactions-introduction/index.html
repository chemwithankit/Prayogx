Key points

  • Burning magnesium gives magnesium oxide: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. The atoms are the same, only rearranged.
  • Signs of a reaction: gas given off, colour change, temperature change, precipitate formed (or a change of state).
  • A balanced equation has the same number of atoms of each element on both sides (law of conservation of mass).
  • Types: combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement, redox. Everyday effects: corrosion (rusting) and rancidity (chips packets are flushed with nitrogen).
