Key points

  • The alpha carbon is the carbon next to the carbonyl carbon; an H on it is an alpha hydrogen.
  • Dilute NaOH removes an alpha H, giving an enolate ion.
  • The enolate attacks the carbonyl carbon of a second molecule: a nucleophilic addition.
  • Ethanal gives the aldol, 3-hydroxybutanal: CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CHO.
  • Heat removes water, giving but-2-enal, CH₃CH=CHCHO.
  • No alpha hydrogen, no aldol: methanal and benzaldehyde do not react this way.
