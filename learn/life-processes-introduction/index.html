Key points

  • Even when we rest, maintenance (repair, upkeep) goes on, so the body needs energy all the time.
  • Nutrition: taking in food. Green plants make it (photosynthesis: autotrophs); we take it in (heterotrophs).
  • Respiration: breaking down food to release energy, mostly with oxygen, in the mitochondria.
  • Transportation carries food, oxygen and wastes (blood pumped by the heart); excretion removes harmful wastes (kidneys).

The four life processes

ProcessWhat it doesExample in humans
NutritionTakes in food (source of energy and materials)Eating and digestion
RespirationReleases energy from foodGlucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + energy (in mitochondria)
TransportationMoves materials to every cellBlood pumped by the heart
ExcretionRemoves harmful metabolic wastesKidneys make urine

Autotrophs and heterotrophs

  • Autotrophs make their own food from simple substances (green plants, by photosynthesis).
  • Heterotrophs depend on food made by others (animals, fungi, most bacteria).
