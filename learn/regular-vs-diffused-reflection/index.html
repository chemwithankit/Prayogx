Regular vs Diffused Reflection

Video not playing? Watch on Instagram · YouTube

A mirror and a white wall both reflect light; only the mirror shows your face. Smooth surfaces keep rays parallel; rough ones scatter them.

Key points

  • Smooth surface → parallel rays stay parallel → image: regular reflection.
  • Rough surface → rays scatter → no image: diffused reflection.
  • Each ray still obeys i = r at its own tiny facet.
  • Diffused reflection lets us see a book from any angle; a wet road becomes smoother and glares.
