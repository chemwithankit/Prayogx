Key points

  • Look at the skeleton first: an open chain, or a ring?
  • Acyclic (open-chain, aliphatic): the chain does not close, as in ethane and butane.
  • Alicyclic: a ring of carbon atoms, as in cyclohexane.
  • Aromatic: benzene and compounds with a benzene ring, such as toluene.
  • Heterocyclic: the ring holds another atom, such as oxygen in furan.
  • Then look at the functional group.

Sorting compounds

CompoundClassWhy
2-MethylpropaneAcyclicA branch closes no ring
CyclohexeneAlicyclicA carbon ring, not a benzene ring
