Key points

  • sp³: 4 single bonds, tetrahedral, about 109.5°, as in methane.
  • sp²: one double bond, trigonal planar, 120°, as in ethene.
  • sp: one triple bond, linear, 180°, as in ethyne.
  • Shortcut: count the groups around carbon: 4 is sp³, 3 is sp², 2 is sp.
  • More s character (25%, 33%, 50%) means shorter, stronger bonds.
