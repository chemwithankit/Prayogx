Key points

  • 360 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 = 2³ × 3² × 5, whichever factor tree you use.
  • Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: every composite number is a product of primes, and this factorisation is unique (apart from the order).
  • HCF = product of the smallest powers of common primes; LCM = product of the greatest powers of all primes. 12 and 18: HCF 6, LCM 36, and HCF × LCM = 12 × 18.
  • √2 cannot be written as p/q (p, q integers, q ≠ 0): it is irrational. The chapter proves √2, √3, √5 irrational by contradiction.

Why √2 is irrational (idea of the proof)

  • Suppose √2 = p/q in lowest terms.
  • Then p² = 2q², so 2 divides p², hence 2 divides p (a prime dividing a square divides the number).
  • Write p = 2r: then q² = 2r², so 2 divides q too.
  • p and q both even contradicts lowest terms, so √2 is irrational.
