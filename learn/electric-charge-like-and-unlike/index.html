Key points

  • Rubbing two bodies transfers electrons from one to the other.
  • Charge is not created: the pair's total stays the same.
  • The body that loses electrons becomes positive; the one that gains them becomes negative.
  • Glass rubbed with silk is positive, and the silk negative. Plastic rubbed with fur is negative.
  • Like charges repel, unlike charges attract.
