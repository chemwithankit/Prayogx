Why Every Organism Needs One Name

Aam, mango, maamidi: one fruit, many names, and one word robin for two different birds. With about 1.8 million species, science needs one name per organism.

Key points

  • Local names change from place to place: one fruit is aam, mango, maamidi, aamba and maavu.
  • One common name can also mean different organisms: the robin of Europe and the robin of America are different species.
  • About 1.7 to 1.8 million species have been described.
  • A scientific name is the same worldwide and belongs to only one organism.
  • Mango is Mangifera indica everywhere in the world.
