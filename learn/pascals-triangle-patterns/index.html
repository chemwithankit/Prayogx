Pascal's Triangle and Number Patterns

One simple rule builds a triangle full of patterns: powers of 2, triangular numbers, Fibonacci and more. And India wrote it down centuries before Pascal.

Key points

  • The rule: each number is the sum of the two numbers above it.
  • Row sums are powers of 2: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16.
  • Diagonals give natural numbers and triangular numbers 1, 3, 6, 10.
  • Slanted lines add up to the Fibonacci numbers.
  • Row n gives the coefficients of (a + b)ⁿ.
  • In India it appears as Meru Prastara, centuries before Pascal.
