Model: particles move faster when hotter (speed ∝ √T), and warm water is also less viscous, so ink diffuses faster: D ∝ T/η (Stokes–Einstein, with the standard viscosity of water). Each particle takes random steps; the spread grows like √(D × time). Sizes and times are not to scale.
