Key points

  • Most materials around us are mixtures: air, sea water, milk, soil.
  • Solution: particles < 1 nm, invisible, never settle (salt water).
  • Suspension: particles > 1000 nm, visible, settle under gravity (muddy water).
  • Colloid: 1-1000 nm, do not settle but scatter light, the Tyndall effect (milk). Separation methods match the mixture: filtration, evaporation, chromatography, distillation, magnetic separation.
