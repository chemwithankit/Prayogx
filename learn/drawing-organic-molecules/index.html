Key points

  • Complete structural formula: every atom and every bond is shown.
  • Condensed formula: bonds are dropped, as in CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₃; repeated groups go in a bracket, CH₃(CH₂)₂CH₃.
  • Bond-line formula: every corner and every line end is a carbon.
  • H atoms on carbon are not written: each carbon has 4 bonds, and the rest are to H.
  • 3D drawing: a solid wedge comes towards you, a dashed wedge goes away, a normal line lies in the plane of the paper.
