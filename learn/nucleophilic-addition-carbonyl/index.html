Key points

  • The C=O bond is polar: carbon is delta plus, oxygen delta minus.
  • A nucleophile attacks the electrophilic carbon; the pi electrons move onto oxygen.
  • The carbon changes from sp² (flat) to sp³ (tetrahedral), giving an alkoxide intermediate.
  • The intermediate takes a proton to give the product: HCN gives a cyanohydrin.
  • Aldehydes react faster than ketones: less crowding, and a more positive carbon.

Why aldehydes react faster

ReasonAldehydeKetone
StericOne alkyl group and a small H: easy to reachTwo alkyl groups: crowded
ElectronicOne electron-releasing group: carbon more positiveTwo electron-releasing groups: carbon less positive
