  1. 1The d- and f-Block Elements: Chapter IntroductionIron bridges, copper wires and bright blue, purple and orange solutions all come from one part of the periodic table. The d-block (groups 3–12) fills d orbitals; the f-block (two rows below) fills f orbitals. Variable oxidation states, colour, magnetism and catalysis make them special. Watch
  2. 2Zinc Is in the d-Block, So Why Is It Not a Transition Metal?A transition element has an incompletely filled d subshell in its atom or in a common ion. Iron (3d⁶) passes; copper (3d¹⁰ atom) passes through Cu²⁺ (3d⁹); zinc stays 3d¹⁰ even as Zn²⁺, so Zn, Cd and Hg are d-block but not transition metals. Watch
  3. 3d-Block Configurations: Why Chromium and Copper Break the RuleThe d-block's general configuration is (n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰ ns¹⁻², with 4s filling before 3d. Chromium (3d⁵4s¹) and copper (3d¹⁰4s¹) move one 4s electron into 3d, because 3d and 4s are very close in energy and half-filled and full d sets are extra stable. Watch
