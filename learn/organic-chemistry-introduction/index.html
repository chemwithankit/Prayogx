Key points

  • Organic chemistry is the chemistry of carbon compounds: medicines, plastics, cotton and DNA are all examples.
  • Carbon compounds number in the millions, so they have a whole branch of chemistry.
  • Carbon forms 4 covalent bonds (tetravalency); methane, CH₄, is tetrahedral.
  • Catenation: carbon atoms bond to each other to form long chains and rings.
  • This chapter teaches how to draw and name molecules, how electrons move in reactions, and how to purify and analyse compounds.
