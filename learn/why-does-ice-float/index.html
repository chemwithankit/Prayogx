Why Does Ice Float?

Key points

  • A solid floats if it is less dense than the liquid.
  • When water freezes, its molecules lock into open hexagons with empty space between them.
  • The mass stays the same but the volume grows about 9%, so density falls to about 0.92 g/cm³.
  • Lakes freeze from the top, so water stays liquid below and fish survive.
  • A floating object's fraction under the surface = its density ÷ the liquid's density.
