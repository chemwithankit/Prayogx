Key points

  • Tollens' reagent is ammoniacal silver nitrate.
  • Aldehydes reduce it, and silver deposits as a silver mirror. The aldehyde becomes a carboxylate.
  • Fehling's solution is blue, with Cu²⁺. Aliphatic aldehydes give a red-brown precipitate of Cu₂O.
  • Ketones do not respond to either test.
  • Aromatic aldehydes such as benzaldehyde give Tollens' but not Fehling's.
