Sub-topics covered

  • Successive ionisation enthalpies and cation configurations

What it teaches

  • The second ionisation removes an electron from the cation, not the atom
  • A filled subshell in the cation makes the next ionisation expensive
  • Within an isoelectronic set the radius falls as the nuclear charge rises
  • Density is a ratio, so a faster-growing volume can outrun a growing mass
  • Lone-pair repulsion on small atoms weakens a single bond
