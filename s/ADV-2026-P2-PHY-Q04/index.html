Period of small radial oscillations of a nudged circular orbit under an inverse-square force

JEE Advanced 2026 Paper 2 · Question 4 Physics Radial oscillation of a nudged circular orbit under an inverse-square force - effective potential and a closed orbit Moderate

A particle on a circular orbit under F = -k/r^2 is nudged outward with its angular momentum unchanged and its distance oscillates. U_eff'' = k/r0^3 with r0 = l^2/(mk) gives T = 2 pi l^3/(mk^2) - option A - the same as the orbital period, so the orbit closes.

Sub-topics covered

  • Central force motion with conserved angular momentum
  • Effective potential U_eff = l^2/(2mr^2) - k/r and the circular orbit at its minimum
  • Small radial oscillations: omega^2 = U_eff''(r0)/m
  • Inverse-square force: radial and orbital periods equal, closed (elliptical) orbits

What it teaches

  • With l fixed the radial motion is that of a particle in U_eff = l^2/(2mr^2) - k/r; the circular orbit is its minimum, r0 = l^2/(mk)
  • U_eff''(r0) = 3l^2/(m r0^4) - 2k/r0^3 = k/r0^3, so omega_r^2 = k/(m r0^3)
  • T = 2 pi sqrt(m r0^3/k) = 2 pi l^3/(m k^2) - option A - equal to the orbital period, so the nudged orbit closes (an ellipse)
  • Option B 2 pi sqrt(m/k) has the wrong units for k in N m^2 (the spring formula); C and D are a third and a fifth of the right value
  • For other power laws k/r^n the radial period is T_orbit/sqrt(3 - n) and the orbit traces a rosette
