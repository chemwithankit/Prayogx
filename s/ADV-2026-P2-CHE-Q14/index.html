What it teaches

  • Cyanide displacement is the only step in the sequence that changes the carbon count, which alone fixes X as a two-carbon acid
  • Red P with Br2 is the Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky reaction and brominates the alpha carbon of an acid
  • Excess ammonia converts an alpha-bromo acid to an alpha-amino acid without alkylating twice
  • A lactam is a cyclic amide; hydrolysis adds a water across the C-N bond and opens the ring, so the product is 18 heavier than the lactam
  • A polyamide needs an amine at one end and an acid at the other, which is why both monomers must be acyclic
  • Condensation polymerisation ejects one small molecule per link, so the polymer is always lighter than the monomers that made it
  • A chain of n units with two loose ends has n minus 1 links, not n - the word acyclic in the question is what makes it 999 rather than 1000
  • Not every number given in a question is used: bromine appears only in an intermediate and never reaches the answer
