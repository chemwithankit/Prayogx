What it teaches

  • Adsorption is a surface process, so nothing leaves the flask - the adsorbate only changes address
  • A pH reading gives the hydrogen ion, not the weak acid that produced it
  • Ka is the conversion factor between [H+] and the concentration of an undissociated weak acid
  • The C in the Freundlich isotherm is the concentration left at equilibrium, never the initial one
  • Taking logarithms turns a power law into a straight line whose gradient is 1/n and whose intercept is log k
