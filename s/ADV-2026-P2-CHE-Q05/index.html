Sub-topics covered

  • Integrated first-order rate law

  • Sign conventions for d[R]/dt and d[P]/dt
  • Half-life independence from initial concentration
  • Arrhenius temperature dependence of k
  • Reading and interpreting kinetics graphs

What it teaches

  • A rate is a slope and carries a sign; a rate constant does not
  • Exponential decay of [R] forces concave-down growth of [P]
  • d[R]/dt is linear in [R] with slope minus k, through the origin
  • k depends on temperature only, never on time or on concentration
  • t-half = ln2/k is the operational signature of first order
