Moment of inertia of four planar rod frames about an axis in their own plane, matched to List-II

JEE Advanced 2026 Paper 1 · Question 16 Physics Moment of inertia of planar rod frames about an axis in their own plane - each rod counts by its perpendicular distance from the axis Easy-Moderate

Four frames of identical rods spin about an axis OCO' in their own plane under the same torque; the slower one spins up, the larger its moment of inertia. Each rod counts by its perpendicular distance from the axis: P ml^2/3 (5), Q 5ml^2/4 (1), R 2ml^2/3 (4), S ml^2/6 (2) - option A.

Sub-topics covered

  • Moment of inertia as the sum of m r_perp^2; only the distance perpendicular to the axis counts
  • A rod with one end on the axis at angle theta: I = (ml^2/3) sin^2 theta
  • A rod parallel to the axis at distance d: I = m d^2; the parallel-axis theorem
  • Rotational dynamics: the same torque gives alpha = tau / I

What it teaches

  • Each rod is integrated along its length: r_perp = s sin theta, I = integral of (m/l)(s sin theta)^2 ds = (ml^2/3) sin^2 theta
  • P two perpendicular rods at 45 deg to the axis: 2 x (ml^2/3)(1/2) = ml^2/3 (5)
  • Q equilateral triangle, axis through C parallel to AB: 2 x (ml^2/3)(3/4) + m(3l^2/4) = 5ml^2/4 (1)
  • R square about its diagonal: 4 x (ml^2/3)(1/2) = 2ml^2/3 (4); S two rods at 30 deg: 2 x (ml^2/3)(1/4) = ml^2/6 (2)
  • P5 Q1 R4 S2: option A; ml^2/12 (3) belongs to no frame
