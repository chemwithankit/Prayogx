Sugars: oxidation, Fehling's test, invert sugar and the rotation of a mixture

JEE Advanced 2026 Paper 2 · Question 9 Chemistry Monosaccharides, reducing sugars and optical rotation Moderate-Hard

Four benches for four statements: oxidise glucose and gluconic acid with HNO3 and get the same product, run Fehling's on a ketose and watch the copper drop out, hydrolyse sucrose into an equimolar pair, then build the average that turns -40 into -20.

Sub-topics covered

  • Gluconic acid and saccharic acid
  • Lobry de Bruyn-van Ekenstein rearrangement

  • Specific rotation of a mixture
  • D/L configuration versus (+)/(-) rotation

What it teaches

  • A strong oxidant attacks every oxidisable end, not just the reducing one
  • Alkali converts a ketose into aldoses through a 1,2-enediol
  • A non-reducing disaccharide has both anomeric carbons tied up
  • Specific rotation of a mixture is a weighted average, never a sum
  • Configuration labels and the measured sign of rotation are independent
