What it teaches

  • Only what is inside the square brackets is bonded to the metal - the chloride written outside is the counter-ion and never reaches a vertex
  • The six sites are numbered the usual way - 1, 2, 3, 4 round the square plane and 5, 6 axial - so the opposite (trans) pairs are 1-3, 2-4 and 5-6 and every other pair is cis
  • An octahedron has 6 vertices, 12 edges and 8 triangular faces, and every face takes exactly one vertex from each opposite pair
  • cis means 90 degrees apart, so two cis donors share an edge; trans means opposite, so no face contains both
  • fac means three mutually cis donors, which form one whole face; mer means one pair is trans and the third is cis to both
  • Every edge of an octahedron lies in exactly two faces, so a Cl-Cl edge contributes two faces carrying two chlorides
  • A count like this depends only on the isomer label, not on which particular vertices are drawn
