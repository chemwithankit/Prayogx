What it teaches

  • Hydrogen-like means exactly one electron, not one proton
  • The Bohr result E = -13.6 Z^2 / n^2 holds only for a one-electron species
  • Dividing two Rydberg equations cancels the constant and the wavelength alike
  • Longer wavelength means lower photon energy, never higher
  • The charge on a hydrogen-like ion is Z - 1, so the question is one subtraction longer than it looks
