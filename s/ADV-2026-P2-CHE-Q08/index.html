What it teaches

  • A protonated amine is deactivating and meta-directing, which is why aniline is acetylated first
  • The acetyl group moderates activation enough to stop nitration at one substitution
  • A diazonium salt couples only with a strongly activated ring
  • Ethanol reduces a diazonium salt and is itself oxidised to an aliphatic aldehyde
  • The phthalein test needs a free position para to the phenolic OH
  • Extended push-pull conjugation moves absorption out of the UV into the visible
