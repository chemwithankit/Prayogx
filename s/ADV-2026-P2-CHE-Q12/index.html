What it teaches

  • Kb is not a table value here - it follows from the solvent's boiling point and enthalpy of vaporisation
  • Quoting the enthalpy of vaporisation as 10R makes the gas constant cancel out of Kb entirely
  • Kb is proportional to the solvent's molar mass and the molality is inversely proportional to it, so their product is independent of the solvent
  • A colligative property counts particles, so B giving 2C + 2D contributes four particles for every one that dissociates
  • i = 1 + 3(alpha) for B into 2C + 2D, bounded by 1 and 4
  • The rise in boiling point is measured from the pure solvent, not the boiling point itself
