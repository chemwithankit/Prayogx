    Understand the concept

    Q — Kolbe, then aromatisation

    Kolbe's electrolysis couples two propyl radicals: n-hexane. V2O5 at 500 °C closes the chain and strips 4 H2.

    2 C3H7COO⁻ → C6H14 → C6H6

    R — Friedel–Crafts acylation

    AlCl3 opens phthalic anhydride to an acylium ion, which acylates benzene.

    R = 2-benzoylbenzoic acid, C14H10O3

    S — Rosenmund reduction

    PCl5 makes the acid chloride; H2 over poisoned Pd/BaSO4 stops at the aldehyde.

    S = 2-benzoylbenzaldehyde, C14H10O2

    T — hydrazine closes a ring

    Both carbonyls of S condense with the two NH2 of hydrazine (−2H2O): a six-membered ring with N–N.

    T = 1-phenylphthalazine, C14H10N2

    1. Step 1 — Q

      2 CH3CH2CH2COO⁻ → CH3(CH2)4CH3 + 2CO2 + 2e⁻ ;  C6H14 —V2O5, 500 °C→ C6H6 + 4H2

      Q = benzene. With excess Cl2 under UV, benzene adds three Cl2 (a free-radical addition, not substitution) to give benzene hexachloride C6H6Cl6; its γ-isomer is gammaxane (lindane), an insecticide. (B) ✓

      On the bench: CO2 bubbled at the anode, the hexane chain folded into a ring in the V2O5 furnace, and six Cl atoms added under the UV lamp.

    2. Step 2 — R, and the (D) trap

      C6H6 + phthalic anhydride —AlCl3→ 2-benzoylbenzoic acid (R)

      Acid (conc. H2SO4) closes R into anthraquinone (−H2O). Zn-Hg/HCl (Clemmensen) turns each C=O into CH2: the product is 9,10-dihydroanthracene, C14H12 — no oxygen at all. 9,10-Dihydroxyanthracene (C14H10O2) is not formed. (D) ✗

    3. Step 3 — S

      R —PCl5→ R–COCl —H2, Pd/BaSO4→ 2-benzoylbenzaldehyde (S)

      S contains a –CHO group, so warming it with Tollens' reagent (ammoniacal AgNO3) reduces Ag⁺ to metallic silver: a silver mirror. (A) ✓

    4. Step 4 — T

      S + NH2NH2 —heat→ 1-phenylphthalazine (T) + 2H2O

      The aldehyde and the ketone are 1,4-related on the ring, so hydrazine bridges them into a six-membered ring containing N=N–C=N… two nitrogen atoms in the ring: T is heterocyclic. (C) ✓

    Independent verification

    • Structures and formulas. Every compound was built with RDKit and its formula counted from its bonds: Q C6H6, R C14H10O3, S C14H10O2, T C14H10N2; each step balances.
    • Reactions as rules. The Friedel–Crafts, Rosenmund, hydrazine and Clemmensen steps were applied as reaction templates to the structures, and the products compared with the named compounds.
    • Functional-group tests. S has an aldehyde C–H (Tollens positive); T has ring nitrogen; the Clemmensen product has no oxygen.
    • Solution websites. MathonGo's published solution (their Q40) gives (A), (B), (C) with the same pathway.
    • This page. Each option is decided from the structures the lab builds; the answer letters are not written into the page.
    • Answer key. Checked afterwards against the official final key printed with the paper: Q8 → (A), (B), (C).
