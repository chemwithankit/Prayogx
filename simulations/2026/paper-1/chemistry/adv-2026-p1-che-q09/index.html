Q.9  Two cylinders, both fitted with frictionless pistons, are filled with mixtures of He and Ar gases. In the first cylinder, the masses of He and Ar are m1 and m2, respectively. In the second cylinder, the masses of He and Ar are m2 and m1, respectively. The molar mass of Ar is 10 times the molar mass of He. The external pressure applied by the piston on the first cylinder needs to be 5 times that on the second cylinder so that the volume of the gas mixtures in both the cylinders are equal at the same temperature. Assuming He and Ar behave like ideal gases, the value of (m1/m2) is ____.

    Understand the concept

    Mass is not moles

    Let MHe = M, so MAr = 10M. A gram of He is ten times as many moles as a gram of Ar.

    n = m / M

    Same V, same T

    For ideal gases PV = nRT. With V and T equal in both cylinders, the pressure is simply proportional to the total moles.

    P₁/P₂ = n₁/n₂

    Set up the equation

    n₁ = m₁/M + m₂/10M and n₂ = m₂/M + m₁/10M. Put n₁ = 5 n₂.

    10m₁ + m₂ = 5(10m₂ + m₁)

    Solve

    5m₁ = 49m₂, so m₁/m₂ = 49/5.

    m₁/m₂ = 49/5

    1. Step 1 — Count the moles

      n₁ = m₁/M + m₂/(10M) = (10m₁ + m₂)/(10M) ;  n₂ = m₂/M + m₁/(10M) = (10m₂ + m₁)/(10M)
      On the bench: every mass m₂ of He appeared as ten small molecules, the same mass of Ar as one large molecule.

    2. Step 2 — Apply the ideal gas law

      P₁V = n₁RT, P₂V = n₂RT ⇒ P₁/P₂ = n₁/n₂ = 5

      R, T and V cancel — the temperature does not matter as long as it is the same for both.

    3. Step 3 — Solve

      (10m₁ + m₂) = 5(10m₂ + m₁) ⇒ 10m₁ − 5m₁ = 50m₂ − m₂ ⇒ 5m₁ = 49m₂
      Answer: m₁/m₂ = —

    4. Step 4 — Check and traps

      n₁/n₂ = (98 + 1)/(10 + 9.8) = 99/19.8 = 5 ✓. Reading the pressure ratio backwards (P₂ = 5P₁) gives 1/9.8 = 0.10; equating masses instead of moles gives no solution at all; taking the pressure ratio itself as the answer gives 5 — all wrong.

    Independent verification

    • Exact algebra. sympy solves (10r + 1) = 5(10 + r) to r = 49/5 = 9.80, and the general form r = (pk − 1)/(k − p).
    • Numerics. A bisection on P₁/P₂(r) − 5, run independently, converges to 9.8000; the page's own scan does the same.
    • Robustness. R, T and V cancel; the real molar masses (4.003 and 39.95 g/mol, k = 9.98) would give 9.82.
    • Solution websites. MathonGo's published solution (their Q41) gives 9.8.
    • This page. The answer is computed at run time from the question's numbers; it is not written into the page.
    • Answer key. Checked afterwards against the official final key printed with the paper: Q9 → 9.80.
